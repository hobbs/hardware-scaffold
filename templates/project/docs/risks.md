# Risk register

Risk is uncertainty that can affect performance, schedule, cost, buildability, or
safety. Hazards also require the process in `docs/domains/safety.md`.

| ID | Failure / uncertainty | Cause | Effect | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation | Verification | Status |

Use qualitative severity (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Critical`) and likelihood
(`Unlikely`, `Possible`, `Likely`) unless the project defines a stronger method.
Residual risk must be visible; a mitigation is not evidence that the risk is gone.
